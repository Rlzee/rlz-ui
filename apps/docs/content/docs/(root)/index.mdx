Build your own UI faster, without starting from scratch.

rlz-ui combines production-ready components with a visual Creator to help you build an interface that matches your product. Start with components that work out of the box, customize their look, and bring your design into your project without building a design system from scratch.

## Why rlz-ui?

- **A solid foundation.** Start with production-ready components built for React, Next.js and Vite.
- **A simpler workflow.** Use the Creator to customize your UI visually and preview changes as you make them, instead of configuring everything by hand.
- **Your own identity.** Shape colors, typography, animations and component styles around your product, while keeping the same foundation.

Use the defaults when they fit, customize what matters, and keep building as your needs evolve.

## From the Creator to your project

The [Creator](/preset/create) makes customization part of getting started, not a separate project.

1. **Customize visually.** Adjust your theme and component styles in one workspace.
2. **Preview as you go.** See your changes on components before bringing them into your app.
3. **Install your preset.** Export your preset, initialize your project with it, and add the components you need through the CLI.

Your preset carries your theme and component customizations into your project. Components you add afterward use that saved preset automatically, so you don't have to repeat the same setup for each one.

## Philosophy

Creating a distinctive interface shouldn't mean rebuilding the basics or spending hours on repetitive configuration.

The goal isn't to replace design systems. It's to make creating and evolving one simpler, with reliable components and tools that shorten the path from an idea to a working interface.

The long-term vision is a platform where every project can build, customize and share its own UI identity. The starting point is practical: less setup, faster customization, and a foundation you can make your own.
